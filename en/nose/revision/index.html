What to consider before revision

  • Which cartilage was used in the previous surgery
  • What you are unhappy with in your current nose, and how you want it to change
  • Whether you have previously had double-jaw surgery or protruding mouth surgery (ASO)
  • The current condition of the nose

What matters especially in revision rhinoplasty is the skin area of the nasal tip. Cartilage and soft tissue inside the nose can be borrowed from elsewhere in the body, but the skin of the nasal tip cannot be borrowed. Nor can it be removed, because of scarring. That is why the skin area of the nasal tip is one of the most important variables in revision rhinoplasty.

When is revision done?

In general, the rule for revision after cosmetic surgery is to wait 6 months to 1 year until the tissue stabilizes, but in modern society it is extremely hard to go without a normal social life for a long time with a damaged appearance. For people suffering like this, at Rococo we correct the nose immediately regardless of timing when there is only a problem with shape and no infection. When there is infection, we remove the implant, stabilize the tissue with 3 weeks of intensive treatment, and then, depending on the condition, carry out a rapid correction using autologous tissue or an implant, so that you can return to your social life.

— Dr. Kim Sang-ho

Which cartilage is best?

My answer to this question is: > there is no single best cartilage <.

(1) Which cartilage was used in the previous surgery.. If a septal extension was done with septal cartilage in the previous surgery and the grafted septal cartilage is still strong and well maintained, this support can be reused, and ear cartilage or AlloDerm can be placed on top to raise the nasal tip further. However, if the grafted cartilage has become soft and weak, septal cartilage can no longer be used. In this case the shape of the nasal tip has to be newly built with rib cartilage or ear cartilage.

— Dr. Kim Sang-ho

Removal surgery and revision rhinoplasty are different surgeries

(2) is revision rhinoplasty, not removal surgery. Saying "make it so the shape doesn't look strange.." clearly carries the hidden meaning "please make it a good-looking shape." Implant / cartilage removal surgery is only a simple removal surgery. It is a surgery that ends with removal, not a surgery that creates a shape.

If you are not prepared to take the risk that the shape may become quite strange, you should not have nasal implant removal / tip cartilage removal surgery; you should have revision rhinoplasty and ask for the shape you want.

— Dr. Kim Sang-ho

METHOD

Surgical method

  1. STEP 01

    Before revision rhinoplasty, it is very important to set specific goals for the surgery before going in.

  2. STEP 02

    Which materials to use and how to operate are decided according to the various conditions of the patient who had surgery and what they want.

  3. STEP 03

    The nasal tip and columella were brought down sufficiently using autologous rib cartilage, and designed to feel as defined and high as possible.

  4. STEP 04

    Before surgery the tip looks turned slightly to the right, and after surgery slightly to the left. When contracture sets in, scar tissue makes the nose hard as a rock. The tissue is so firm that it is difficult even to inject anesthetic during surgery. That is why revision of a contracted nose like this is the most difficult surgery even within revision rhinoplasty. The surgeon needs a wide range of revision experience and many tools to produce a proper result.

In upturned nose correction, rather than focusing on fine lines, I give priority to correcting the upturned nose firmly with a strong structure so that it recurs as little as possible. While doing that, I refine the line as much as I can,

but because there is a lot of scar tissue and not enough slack in the tissue, there may be some shortfalls in detail after surgery. These are corrected as what we call a minor touch, which I take responsibility for, after 3 months, once the wound has stabilized to some degree.

RECOVERY

Recovery

  1. If there was infection

    First, the plan was to remove the implant and do a rapid corrective rhinoplasty 3 weeks later.

  2. 7 days (pinched nose correction)

    Skin-colored tape is placed only on the nasal tip for 7 days. Stitches are removed on day 7.

  3. 3 months

    These are corrected as what we call a minor touch, which I take responsibility for, after 3 months, once the wound has stabilized to some degree.

  4. 6 months

    The shape has been well maintained up to 6 months, when it enters the stable phase.

Can the shape of the nose be predicted after the implant is removed?

And what the nose will look like after removal can never be predicted. So some noses look surprisingly fine after removal, while in other cases the shape becomes quite unattractive, with the nose twisting or turning up.

From Dr. Kim's column (Korean) →
Why does the nose turn up and become distorted after infection?

When an infection occurs after nose surgery and it lasts a long time, or an infection that can't be seen continues, contracture develops. Typically the nose becomes distorted and upturned. The nasal tip may become asymmetric, and the skin of the tip may become uneven.

From Dr. Kim's column (Korean) →
Can revision rhinoplasty be done with donor rib cartilage?

Without rib cartilage it is hard to make this much change, and I think autologous rib cartilage is the best material. Many people ask for donor rib cartilage, but I currently do not use donor rib cartilage.
