TYPE · ALAR LOWERING

There are four main reasons nostrils show

  • An upturned nose
  • The upper edge of the nostril is raised (M-shaped nostrils)
  • The lower edge of the nostril is lowered (V-shaped nostrils)
  • Large nostrils

Usually two or more of the causes above are present at the same time.

DOCTOR'S NOTE

Dr. Kim's notes

A surgery often sought by people whose nostrils show

Some people are born with too little soft tissue covering the nostrils, so their nostrils show from the front. When the nostrils are exposed like this, it doesn't look good, and in face reading it is also said to be a feature that keeps wealth from gathering, so many of our clients want it corrected.

In some people, part of the alar cartilage at the nasal tip is congenitally underdeveloped and the soft tissue on the side of the nostril is lacking, so the nostrils look triangular. Seen together, the two sides look like the letter M, so I personally call these M-shaped nostrils.

— Dr. Kim Sang-ho

Three types of M-shaped nostrils

M-shaped nostrils can be divided into three main types. I classify them as type 1, 2 and 3 depending on whether the raised peak of the nostril is located toward the inside, in the middle, or toward the outside.

M-shaped nostril Type 1 is when the upward-pointing peak of the nostril is gathered toward the inside. Of the three types, this is the type in which alar rim lowering works best.

M-shaped nostril Type 2 is when the upward-pointing peak of the nostril is located in the middle. Of the three types, this is the type in which alar rim lowering has a moderate effect.

M-shaped nostril Type 3 is when the upward-pointing peak of the nostril is spread toward the outside. Of the three types, this is the type in which alar rim lowering is least effective.

— Dr. Kim Sang-ho

Not every M-shaped nostril needs correction

Having M-shaped nostrils doesn't mean they look unattractive in every case. Depending on the shape of the nostril floor, the nostrils may show more, or in some cases a moderate amount of nostril show actually has the advantage of making the nasal tip look smaller.

In other words, not every M-shaped nostril needs to be corrected. The most important thing is your own subjective judgment.

— Dr. Kim Sang-ho

METHOD

Surgical method

  1. STEP 01

    When you wake up, the surgery is already over.

  2. STEP 02

    The surgery uses a composite graft that transplants ear cartilage and skin together,

  3. STEP 03

    with a third-generation alar rim lowering method that minimizes, as much as possible, the bulky nasal tip that is the drawback of this approach.

  4. STEP 04

    When only one side is done, the surgery takes about 20 minutes.

  5. STEP 05

    After surgery, skin-colored tape is placed on the nasal tip for about a week.

From Dr. Kim's column (Korean) →

In this method, cartilage + skin tissue is taken from both ears and grafted inside the nostrils. It is done under sedation and takes about 40 minutes.

From Dr. Kim's column (Korean) →

Because the skin of the nasal tip has to be brought down to cover the nostrils, the nasal tip inevitably becomes larger. In particular, for up to 3 months after surgery, postoperative swelling adds to this and the nasal tip looks even bulkier.

Even people who were worried are mostly satisfied once the swelling goes down, but roughly one person every 2–3 years wants part or all of the graft removed to reduce the volume of the nasal tip.

RECOVERY

Recovery

  1. Day of surgery

    When you wake up, the surgery is already over.

    There is no pain at all.

  2. 1 week

    After surgery, skin-colored tape is placed on the nasal tip for about a week.

    After the tape is removed you can wash your face and wear makeup, and although there is still some swelling, most people go about their daily lives.

  3. 2 weeks

    Usually after about 2 weeks you can go about daily life without much trouble, but depending on the nose shape or constitution, early swelling is rarely severe and lasts a long time.

  4. 2 months

    Alcohol and smoking are strictly forbidden for 2 months after surgery; other than that, there is nothing in particular to be careful about.

    Until the grafted skin has fully taken, you need to apply ointment well to keep it moist, and simply come in on your scheduled treatment dates.

  5. Swelling

    Swelling of the nasal tip keeps going down for 6–12 months.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can alar rim lowering be removed?

<< Alar rim lowering can be removed regardless of timing. >> However, I don't recommend partial removal in the early period after surgery except in very special cases. This is because after a partial removal, the remaining graft may also die (necrosis), and the whole graft may end up having to be removed.

From Dr. Kim's column (Korean) →
Will a scar show inside the nostril after surgery?

In 97% of patients, the scar inside the nostril is hard to notice. In about 3%, especially in a "dragon nose," the skin graft area may look like a scar when seen from below.

From Dr. Kim's column (Korean) →
Can alar rim lowering be done on a dragon nose (Type 3)?

In Type 3 nostrils with a dragon-nose shape, alar rim lowering covers the nostrils in an unattractive way. For a dragon nose without much nostril show from the front, I do not recommend alar rim lowering.

From Dr. Kim's column (Korean) →
Does taking cartilage and skin from the ear change the shape of the ear?

After ear cartilage + skin is harvested, the ear becomes slightly shorter and sits a little closer to the head. So far I have never received a complaint about a change in ear shape.

From Dr. Kim's column (Korean) →
Will breathing become uncomfortable after surgery?

Because the surgery is done at the entrance of the nostril, there is a theoretical possibility that it could affect breathing even slightly. I have not yet seen a case where breathing discomfort after alar rim lowering alone became a problem.
