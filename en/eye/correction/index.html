Ptosis Correction (Eye-Opening Correction)

How is ptosis correction done?

It is a surgery that strengthens the eye-opening power so that the eyes open bright and wide.

  • 10–20minSurgery time (no-crease correction · non-incisional, both eyes)
  • 5–7daysRecovery period (about 2 weeks if bruising occurs, rarely)

Adjusting the eye-opening muscle so the eyes open better

Ptosis correction is a surgical method for people whose eye-opening power is weak: the eye-opening muscle is adjusted so the eyes open better, making them bigger and clearer.

It was originally done with an incisional method, but a few years ago non-incisional ptosis correction was developed and has been giving good results.

— Dr. Kim Sang-ho

Non-incisional and incisionless (no-crease) correction

Without an incision, the double eyelid is made through needle holes in a way similar to the buried suture method, and the eye-opening muscle is tied with a suture from the inside.

Incisionless ptosis correction is a surgery that only strengthens the eye-opening muscle, without leaving even a needle hole in the skin.

It is a method applied when someone who already has double eyelids just wants their eyes to open more clearly.. or when someone without double eyelids wants their eyes to open clearly without creating a double eyelid.

The only difference between non-incisional and incisionless ptosis correction is that non-incisional correction adds making a double eyelid through needle holes.

— Dr. Kim Sang-ho

Why I stopped doing incisional correction

That kind of incisional ptosis correction, first of all, leaves a scar, swelling is naturally more severe, if there is an error in the surgery the corrective revision also requires a new incision, and if the correction is overdone it becomes very hard to fix — in a word, it was a very difficult, high-risk surgery.

I perform quite a lot of ptosis correction, and as far as I remember, I have not done incisional ptosis correction even once in the last 3 years or more. Such surgery has become almost unnecessary for me now.

On the contrary, there have been quite a lot of cases where people who had incisional ptosis correction at another clinic were left with severe asymmetry or still lacked eye-opening power, and I achieved good results by revising them with non-incisional ptosis correction.

— Dr. Kim Sang-ho

When the double eyelid fades or forms multiple folds

In people with protruding eyes or a hollow upper eyelid area, problems such as the double eyelid fading or forming multiple folds (a triple fold) often occur.

Unlike in ordinary eyes, the direction in which the eye opens and the direction of the eyelid skin are similar, so instead of one deep double eyelid fold, many shallow fine folds form. The skin gets crinkled like an accordion.

When eye-opening power is weak, the multiple-fold problem gets worse, so it is good to do ptosis correction together, or even on its own.

— Dr. Kim Sang-ho

METHOD

Surgical steps

  1. STEP 01

    Local anesthesia is given under sedation, so it is a painless surgery,

  2. STEP 02

    the double eyelid is made with the natural adhesion method, and the muscle inside the eyelid is tied with a suture to strengthen it.

  3. STEP 03

    Because the double eyelid line is made with the natural adhesion method, only needle-hole-sized scars remain, which in most cases are also barely noticeable.

  4. STEP 04

    The surgery takes 20 minutes.

  5. STEP 05

    You can expect a recovery period of about 5–7 days, but rarely, bruising occurs when the anesthetic is injected, and it can last up to about 2 weeks.

If I had to name a drawback of non-incisional ptosis correction.. it is that the strength may be slightly weaker than with the incisional method, but most eyes, except those that pathologically cannot open well, can be corrected sufficiently with the non-incisional method, which is why non-incisional ptosis correction has become quite popular in the last few years.

When non-incisional ptosis correction is done on asymmetric eyes, it is hard to correct the asymmetry 100% with one surgery. In about 10% of my patients, one in ten, some asymmetry remains after surgery, and in such cases I usually do a re-correction around 3–4 weeks, when the swelling has mostly gone down. The eye that opens less is simply lifted slightly with the no-crease ptosis correction method.

With the incisional method, such an additional correction was also quite a burden, but with the non-incisional method it is a simple procedure taking about 5 minutes, so you can have it with an incomparably smaller burden.

RECOVERY

Recovery

  1. 1–2 days

    For about 1–2 days after surgery, there can be quite a bit of swelling.

  2. 5 days

    Men usually have more bruising and swelling than women.. The eyelids are still swollen, but it is at a level where daily life is possible.

  3. 5–7 days

    You can expect a recovery period of about 5–7 days, but rarely, bruising occurs when the anesthetic is injected, and it can last up to about 2 weeks.

  4. Double eyelid line

    The line looks strong because there is still swelling; as the swelling goes down more, it changes into a natural, soft line.

  5. Early asymmetry

    Because the muscles that open the two eyes affect each other, when ptosis correction is done in someone with severe asymmetry, asymmetry may be visible early on, but in most cases it is corrected as the swelling goes down.

Swelling and bruising vary a lot between individuals, and a bit of luck at the time also plays a part.

RELATED PROCEDURES

Related procedures

Fat grafting for hollow eyes

As people age, some develop a sunken area above the eyes, giving a hollow, gaunt look. Of course, some people have had it since they were young. In such cases, very good results can be achieved with fat grafting.

Lower eyelid fat repositioning

The shadow below the eye appears because the fat pouch surrounding the orbital fat weakens and the fat inside bulges out; when this shadow deepens, a person looks tired and older and gives a poor impression. Lower eyelid fat repositioning is a surgery that removes this lower shadow.

Upper & lower blepharoplasty

As we age, signs of aging appear on the face, and the most noticeable are the changes around the eyes. The outer third or so of the eyebrow droops down so the upper eyelid blocks the field of vision, and to compensate, the forehead muscle is used to pull the eyebrows up. As a result, deep wrinkles form on the forehead. Surgeries that correct this aging include upper blepharoplasty, sub-brow lift and forehead lift above the eyes, and lower blepharoplasty below the eyes.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can eyes that had incisional ptosis correction be revised with the non-incisional method?

Some doctors say that non-incisional ptosis correction can't be done on eyes that had incisional ptosis correction, but that is no~t true at all. Over the last 5 years or so, I have been getting good results by revising, with non-incisional ptosis correction, many patients who did not get good results from incisional ptosis correction.

Doesn't non-incisional ptosis correction loosen sooner than the incisional method?

I also had my doubts.. whether the non-incisional method lasts a shorter time than the incisional method, but after following the results for about 5 years, I have concluded that there is almost no difference, and I confidently recommend the surgery to patients who need it.

Can it be done without looking operated on, for men too?

For men, the surgery is mostly done as an inner (hidden) double eyelid, so it can be done so that it barely looks operated on. Because the double eyelid line is made with the natural adhesion method, only needle-hole-sized scars remain, which in most cases are also barely noticeable.

What happens if incisionless ptosis correction is done on eyes that already have a double eyelid?

If eyes that already had a double eyelid have incisionless ptosis correction, the eyes open bigger, and the double eyelid may become somewhat smaller. Looked at the other way, this becomes a very big advantage: when a double eyelid is so large that it looks operated on or feels too much, its size can be reduced in a simple way.

Does ptosis correction help eyes with a hollow upper eyelid?

Weak eye-opening power is one of the causes of hollow eyes. Upper eyelid fat grafting and no-crease ptosis correction are both surgeries that increase volume above the eye, and at the same time they make the double eyelid slightly lower.
