Rear Cheekbone Reduction

How is rear cheekbone reduction done?

Among various unusual shapes, some people have a bulge that sticks out like a lump at the sideburn area right in front of the ear.. Patients call this area the "rear cheekbone".

  • 15–20minSurgery time (both sides)
  • 3–5daysReturn to daily life
  • 6–12monthsUntil the scar fades to a faint line

TYPE · REAR CHEEKBONE

In fact, in most patients that rear cheekbone area is not very noticeable.

  • When the skin over the rear cheekbone is thin and the rear cheekbone pops out as a bulge, I recommend having rear cheekbone reduction together with modified 15-minute cheekbone reduction.
  • On an X-ray it looks like it bulges out like that, but the flesh actually covering that area is quite thick, so it doesn't look bulging from the outside.
  • However, when the flesh covering that bone is thin, or the surrounding tissue can't wrap around it, people whose rear cheekbone pops out like a lump can see a good effect from rear cheekbone reduction.

So rear cheekbone reduction is not a surgery that helps in every case.

What is the "rear cheekbone"?

It is not an official term. Anatomically speaking, it is the root portion of the zygomatic process of the temporal bone..

Sometimes the rear cheekbone remains after cheekbone reduction, making the cheekbone reduction less effective, or in severe cases.. the rear cheekbone stands out as a bulge, like a lump.

— Dr. Kim Sang-ho

The face doesn't change as much as the bone is reduced

Personally, I think not that many people need rear cheekbone reduction. In most cases the rear cheekbone area is not much different from the width of the skull and is covered with thick flesh, so even if you reduce it, there is almost no change on the outside. In other words, reducing the rear cheekbone a lot on an X-ray does not necessarily change your face in the mirror from the front by that much. So I recommend the surgery only to those who really need it.

— Dr. Kim Sang-ho

METHOD

Surgical steps

  1. STEP 01

    It is a simple surgery that takes about 15 minutes under sedation.

  2. STEP 02

    An incision of about 1.5–2 cm is made behind the sideburns, and the rear cheekbone area is smoothed through it.

  3. STEP 03

    For those who want rear cheekbone reduction, I smooth only the red-marked border area.

  4. STEP 04

    Because the jaw joint has to be protected, it cannot be made completely flat, so a slight bulging feel may remain after surgery,

  5. STEP 05

    but in people whose bone sticks out a lot, a noticeably softer change can still be made.

From Dr. Kim's column (Korean) →

To remove the rear cheekbone, a bone-shaving instrument called a burr is usually used, but in the area of the incision in front of the ear there is the facial nerve going to the forehead and a very large artery running up from the neck to the scalp, so there is a risk of damaging these important structures when using a burr. That is why I remove the bone of the rear cheekbone very safely using an instrument I designed myself.

From Dr. Kim's column (Korean) →

That rear cheekbone area is a very important part that makes up part of our jaw joint (temporomandibular joint). Part of the joint capsule surrounding the jaw joint is attached to the rear cheekbone, so if all the bone is removed as in the photo above, jaw joint problems can develop.

In cases where this could affect the result, I explain it in advance during the consultation: that the skull bone may remain and be noticeable after surgery..

RECOVERY

Recovery

  1. 3–5 days

    The swelling is not that severe, so most patients return to daily life within 3–5 days.

  2. 2 weeks

    2 weeks after modified 15-minute cheekbone reduction + rear cheekbone reduction. You can see the rear cheekbone area has been reduced quite a lot.

  3. 3 weeks

    The side cheekbone and rear cheekbone that stuck out on the right side of the face were smoothed out with modified 15-minute cheekbone reduction + rear cheekbone surgery. As the residual swelling goes down further, the line becomes even slimmer.

  4. 6–12 months

    How much of a scar remains varies from person to person, but after about 6–12 months it mostly remains only as a faint line that is hardly noticeable, so you don't need to worry particularly about the scar.

  5. 1 year

    Residual swelling goes down for up to a year, so the volume of the cheekbone area decreases further after that.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much of a scar does rear cheekbone reduction leave?

Rear cheekbone reduction is done through a vertical incision of about 1.5–2 cm at the border behind the sideburns. So a thin linear scar remains; how much of a scar remains varies from person to person, but after about 6–12 months it mostly remains only as a faint line that is hardly noticeable, so you don't need to worry particularly about the scar.

From Dr. Kim's column (Korean) →
How much can the rear cheekbone be reduced?

This varies so much between individuals that it is hard to give a definite answer. In other words, reducing the rear cheekbone a lot on an X-ray does not necessarily change your face in the mirror from the front by that much.

From Dr. Kim's column (Korean) →
Does everyone need the rear cheekbone reduced?

First of all, rear cheekbone reduction does not help in every case. The surgery is meaningful for people whose bone at the sideburn area sticks out unusually, like a lump.

From Dr. Kim's column (Korean) →
Can the rear cheekbone be removed completely?

Even with rear cheekbone reduction, there is a limit to how much can be reduced in order to protect the jaw joint, so it cannot be removed completely; I reduce it only to the extent of smoothing it out.

From Dr. Kim's column (Korean) →
I had cheekbone reduction at another clinic and the rear cheekbone remains. Can I have surgery on just the rear cheekbone?

For rear cheekbone reduction, the side cheekbone first has to be pushed in sufficiently. Rear cheekbone reduction cannot be done when the side cheekbone has not been reduced enough. So for those who come after surgery at another clinic with insufficient side cheekbone reduction, I perform rear cheekbone reduction together with revision cheekbone surgery, and when another clinic has separately fixed the back part of the zygomatic arch with plates and screws, I remove the plates and screws and reduce the rear cheekbone at the same time.
