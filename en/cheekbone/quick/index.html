Quick Cheekbone Reduction (15-Minute Cheekbone Reduction)

How is quick (15-minute) cheekbone reduction done?

"Rococo 15-minute cheekbone reduction" reduces only the side cheekbones (quick cheekbone reduction), while "Rococo modified 15-minute cheekbone reduction + Rococo mini lifting" reduces both the side cheekbones and the 45-degree cheekbones.

  • 10–20minSurgery time (quick · modified 15-minute)
  • 1–2weeksTypical recovery for modified 15-minute reduction
  • 17yearsYears performing quick cheekbone reduction

The quick cheekbone method is not defined in any textbook, but in general you can think of it as

  • cheekbone reduction done under sedation,
  • without an incision inside the mouth,
  • using a natural fixation method

Side cheekbones and 45-degree cheekbones

15-minute cheekbone reduction is a surgery specialized for the "side cheekbones"; when the "45-degree cheekbones" stick out a lot, the modified 15-minute cheekbone reduction can also reduce the volume in the 45-degree area fairly safely.

The 45-degree cheekbone below the outer corner of the eye remains. To reduce this area as well, you need the modified 15-minute cheekbone reduction, done with the quick method.

— Dr. Kim Sang-ho

The cheekbone area is not made by the bone alone

However, the area we call the "cheekbones" on the face is not a structure made simply by the "cheekbone (zygomatic bone)" alone. The cheekbone + the soft tissue (flesh) covering it + its relationship with the structures around it — broadly, these three factors determine the shape of the cheekbone area, and the important surrounding structures are three: the temples, the eyes, and the cheeks including the lower face (deep cheeks).

In cases like this, cheekbone surgery alone has limits in creating a good result. Only by filling more volume into the lower face to wrap around the cheekbone area can a soft-looking face be created.

— Dr. Kim Sang-ho
Read the original (Korean)

하지만, 우리가 얼굴에서 <광대>라고 부르는 부위는 단순히 <광대뼈>만으로 만들어지는 구조물이 아니랍니다. 광대뼈+광대뼈를 덮고 있는 연부조직(살) + 광대주변의 구조물과의 관계 크게 보면 이 세가지 요인이 광대의 형태를 결정하게 되는데요, 광대주변의 중요 구조물로는 관자, 눈, 하관을 포함한 볼(심부볼)의 세가지가 있습니다.

이런 경우는 광대뼈 수술만으로는 좋은 결과를 만드는 데 한계가 있습니다. 반드시 하관에 볼룸을 더 채워서 광대부위를 감싸줘야 부드러운 얼굴을 만들 수 있답니다.

How quick cheekbone reduction came about

I have been very interested in cheekbone surgery since my residency, and through constant effort, starting from cheekbone reduction through intraoral and preauricular (in front of the ear) incisions, and then going through endoscopic techniques, I developed the method little by little until I created the current 15-minute cheekbone reduction (quick cheekbone) method myself.

When I first performed cheekbone reduction through intraoral and preauricular incisions, it took more than an hour, and when I first did endoscopic surgery it also needed more than an hour of surgery time. Such a long surgery time leads to severe swelling and becomes a decisive factor in delaying the patient's return to daily life.

In the end, the surgery time was reduced from an hour to 15 minutes, and the incision length from 3 cm to 1.5 cm.

— Dr. Kim Sang-ho

저는 전공의 시절부터 광대수술에 관심이 많았고, 부단한 노력 끝에 입안절개와 귀앞절개를 통한 광대축소로 시작하여, 내시경을 이용한 수술법을 거치며 조금씩 수술법을 발전시켜 지금의 15분 광대축소술 (퀵광대) 수술법을 직접 개발하게 되었습니다.

제가 처음 입안절개와 귀앞 절개를 통해 광대축소술을 할 때 1시간이 넘게 걸렸고, 처음 내시경 수술을 할 때도 1시간 넘는 수술시간이 필요했습니다. 이러한 긴 수술시간은 심한 붓기로 이어지고 고객의 사회복귀를 늦추는 결정적인 요인이 됩니다.

METHOD

Surgical steps

  1. STEP 01

    The surgery is done under sedation.

  2. STEP 02

    It is done through a really small 1.5 cm incision in the scalp (where hair grows) above the ear.

  3. STEP 03

    The cut bone is checked with an endoscope, so you don't need to worry.

  4. STEP 04

    It is done without fixation pins, so even if you happen to have an X-ray taken later in life, no one except medical staff would know you had surgery.

  5. STEP 05

    After Rococo 15-minute cheekbone reduction, we attach a special sponge that helps prevent bruising and swelling. It is best to keep this sponge on for 3 days.

From Dr. Kim's column (Korean) →

I explain this in advance before surgery: even with 15-minute cheekbone reduction, about 1–2 in 100 people get a lot of bruising and swelling after surgery. There may also be about a 1-in-1,000 chance of severe bruising. This is because the human body cannot be predicted 100%, so it is best to take this into account when planning your surgery schedule.

For 45-degree cheekbones, the method many people know — removing part of the cheekbone through an incision inside the mouth under general anesthesia and fixing it with metal plates and screws — gives the best results.

During consultations I tell patients that, conservatively, the effect of modified 15-minute cheekbone reduction is about 50% of that of surgery under general anesthesia. An effect of 50% means comparing the least effective result of modified 15-minute cheekbone surgery with the best result of intraoral surgery under general anesthesia.

RECOVERY

Recovery

  1. Quick (side cheekbones)

    For Rococo 15-minute cheekbone reduction, the surgery takes about 10 minutes, and in about 98–99% of cases (98–99 out of 100 people) you can expect a typical recovery period of about 2–3 days. There may be slight bruising and swelling around the eyes, but it is at a level that makeup can cover.

  2. Modified 15-minute (incl. 45-degree)

    For Rococo modified 15-minute cheekbone reduction + Rococo mini lifting, the surgery takes about 20 minutes, and in about 99% of cases (99 out of 100 people) you can expect a typical recovery period of about 1–2 weeks; it is best to plan your surgery allowing a generous 2 weeks of recovery. In about 1–2% (one or two in a hundred), bruising and swelling may last more than 2 weeks.

  3. Sponge

    After Rococo 15-minute cheekbone reduction, we attach a special sponge that helps prevent bruising and swelling. It is best to keep this sponge on for 3 days.

    After Rococo modified 15-minute cheekbone reduction, you leave the clinic with a sponge on your face to prevent swelling and bruising, and we recommend keeping the sponge on for 7 days.

  4. Next day – 1 month

    You can wash your hair and shower from the day after surgery. You visit once on day 3 after surgery to have the stitches in the scalp removed, and come back at one month for a follow-up.

  5. Swelling

    The 45-degree area shows its effect later than the side cheekbones. The basic swelling goes down over 3 months, and after that subtle swelling keeps going down for up to a year as the volume decreases.

From Dr. Kim's column (Korean) →

Around 1 month after surgery there is a change you can feel yourself; a photo at 3 months looks quite different from the 1-month photo, and a photo at 1 year shows the area even more reduced than at 3 months. This is because subtle residual swelling keeps going down.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Will my cheeks sag 10 years after cheekbone reduction?

When the volume of the cheekbones, especially the 45-degree cheekbones, is reduced, you have to assume that cheek sagging increases accordingly. What matters is whether that sagging is clinically visible to the eye. That is why I currently reduce the volume of the 45-degree cheekbones only to the extent that no noticeable sagging occurs.

From Dr. Kim's column (Korean) →
If the bone isn't fixed (natural fixation), will it come back out?

That is a marketing rumor created by clinics that do fixation surgery. I have already reduced the cheekbones of countless patients with 15-minute cheekbone reduction for 4 years now; if the cheekbones had come back, this surgery would have disappeared long ago. With 15-minute cheekbone reduction, the cheekbones never come back after surgery, so you can rest assured.

From Dr. Kim's column (Korean) →
Where is the scar, and what is it like?

In the (modified) 15-minute cheekbone reduction I perform, the scar was originally a very small 1–1.5 cm incision scar inside the scalp. For female patients, even if a small scar remains, it is hidden by the hair and is nearly invisible in more than 99% of patients, but in men who keep the sides of their head shaved very short, a small scar may be visible, like a little cut made for style by hip-hop artists.

From Dr. Kim's column (Korean) →
Without fixation, isn't there a risk of non-union (the bone not healing)?

Because it is a natural fixation surgery without separate medical wire or plate-and-screw fixation, many people wonder whether non-union can occur, but so far there has not been a single case of non-union. It is a very safe surgery.

From Dr. Kim's column (Korean) →
Can cheekbone surgery correct facial asymmetry?

Also, many people ask about correcting asymmetry, but facial asymmetry is not corrected by cheekbone surgery. Please keep this in mind.
